Attendance policy

Monthly participation is calculated as attended eligible Sundays ÷ eligible Sundays. Profiles become active at 60% or more. Paused members are excluded from the denominator while paused.

For fair records, register one check-in per member per Sunday. Use the export feature to keep a backup.

Faith & community

Events calendar

Kenya public holidays and common Christian/community observances. Confirm dates with the official ACK parish calendar.

Movable Christian dates (Easter and Pentecost) vary by year. Mother's Day, Father's Day, Valentine's Day, International Girlfriend's Day and National Boyfriend Day are community observances, not official ACK or Kenyan public holidays. Passover follows the Jewish calendar. Mourning or memorial observances should be added only when formally announced.

Keep your records safe

Data & settings

Export backups before changing device or browser. Local backup plus optional sync to the existing Google Apps Script server.

Backup and restore

Download a JSON backup containing member profiles, attendance, finances, trivia scores, setlist and events. The file can include member photos, so keep it private.

Storage & server status

For real deployment: use Firebase or another backend, server-enforced roles, consent and privacy notices, backups, and an audit log. Never store raw fingerprint or face data in localStorage.
