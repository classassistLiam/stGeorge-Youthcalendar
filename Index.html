Attendance check-in

Select a registered member and confirm their identity.

A normal webpage cannot read a phone's fingerprint sensor or a laptop's fingerprint pad directly. This demo records a touch confirmation only; it does not claim a biometric match.

Attendance policy

Monthly participation is calculated as attended eligible Sundays ÷ eligible Sundays. Profiles become active at 60% or more. Paused members are excluded from the denominator while paused.

For fair records, register one check-in per member per Sunday. Use the export feature to keep a backup.
